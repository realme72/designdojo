# Premium economy

A new cabin, `PREMIUM_ECONOMY`, sits between economy and business. Its seats are numbered
`P1, P2, …`. Flights list its seat count and base fare in `seats` / `fares` like the other
classes (a flight without it simply has 0 such seats). Search, booking, dynamic fares and
refunds work for it exactly as for the other cabins.

*Interviewer's question:* how many places know the list of cabins?
